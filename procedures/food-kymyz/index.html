Full procedure Sell kymyz from a roadside stand

Fresh kymyz from your own mares, sold in season from a stand by the road.

Kymyz may be sold from a seasonal stand by a herder with a trading patent, a medical book and a veterinary certificate for the mares. Kymyz under 2.5% alcohol is not an alcoholic drink for licensing, which is shown by a monthly certificate. The season runs from the blessing of the mare's milk in late May to September.

Have the mares tested

Brucellosis and tuberculosis tests by the district station of the State Veterinary Inspectorate.

  1. 1. Call the district veterinarian to the herd
    Where: State Veterinary Inspectorate
    The test is taken at the summer camp. If the camp is across the Qara-Chik line, the mares are tested on the ISEZ side before they go up.
    Bring: ID card or passport of the applicant
    Cost: IS 35 per mare
    Time: 0 min in the queue, 30–90 min at the counter, 7–14 days until the next step
  2. 2. Pay the certificate fee in cash
    Where: Cash desk of the Agricultural Directorate
    The fee is paid in cash som only, at the cash desk of the Agricultural Directorate in the old building on Tokmok Street, and not at the Inspectorate. Bring the cash receipt back to the Inspectorate, where the stamp is given.
    Cost: IS 45 (cash som only)
    Time: 20–70 min in the queue, 5–10 min at the counter, 0–1 days until the next step
  3. 3. Collect the veterinary certificate for the mares (brucellosis and tuberculosis)
    Where: State Veterinary Inspectorate
    You receive: Veterinary certificate for the mares (brucellosis and tuberculosis)
    Time: 30–120 min in the queue, 5–10 min at the counter, 0–1 days until the next step

Have the alcohol content certified

Certificate of Iradstandart that the kymyz contains less than 2.5% alcohol and is therefore not an alcoholic drink for licensing.

  1. 4. Bring a one-litre sample to the laboratory
    Where: Iradstandart, State Agency for Standards, Metrology and Certification
    Kymyz grows stronger as it stands. The certificate is valid for 30 days and applies to kymyz no older than two days; older kymyz is not covered by it.
    Bring: Veterinary certificate for the mares (brucellosis and tuberculosis)
    You receive: Certificate of alcohol content (kymyz under 2.5%)
    Cost: monthly test, IS 75 per month
    Time: 30–90 min in the queue, 10–15 min at the counter, 3–5 days until the next step

Have the kymyz tested each month

Monthly microbiological test at the City Sanitary Inspectorate's laboratory.

  1. 5. Bring a sample in a sterile bottle
    Where: City Sanitary Inspectorate
    Bring: Veterinary certificate for the mares (brucellosis and tuberculosis)
    You receive: Laboratory report on the kymyz (monthly)
    Cost: IS 60 per month
    Time: 30–90 min in the queue, 10–15 min at the counter, 3–5 days until the next step

Obtain a medical book

Medical commission at the city polyclinic or the district hospital.

  1. 6. Pass the medical commission
    Where: City polyclinic (medical commissions)
    Bring: ID card or passport of the applicant, Two photographs, 3.5 × 4.5 cm
    You receive: Medical book of the seller
    Cost: IS 140
    Time: 60–240 min in the queue, 60–120 min at the counter, 5–10 days until the next step

Obtain a permit for the trading place

Seasonal permit of the district administration, with the consent of the Roads Department if the stand is near a state road.

  1. 7. Obtain the consent of the Roads Department
    Where: Roads Department
    Needed if the stand is within 25 m of a state road. A stand may not be placed within 150 m of a Gendarmerie control post, or on the Fergana Gateway road above the snow-closure gate.
    Bring: ID card or passport of the applicant
    You receive: Consent of the Roads Department (within 25 m of a state road)
    Cost: IS 40
    Time: 30–90 min in the queue, 10–15 min at the counter, 7–14 days until the next step
  2. 8. Apply for the seasonal trading place
    Where: District administration: trade and agriculture section
    Bring: Consent of the Roads Department (within 25 m of a state road), Medical book of the seller, Veterinary certificate for the mares (brucellosis and tuberculosis), ID card or passport of the applicant
    You receive: Permit for a seasonal trading place by the road
    Cost: IS 30 per month
    Time: 30–120 min in the queue, 10–15 min at the counter, 5–10 days until the next step

Buy a seasonal trading patent

Patent of the District Tax Inspectorate in place of registration as a sole trader.

  1. 9. Buy the patent for the months of the season
    Where: District Tax Inspectorate
    Bring: Permit for a seasonal trading place by the road, ID card or passport of the applicant
    You receive: Seasonal trading patent
    Cost: IS 55 per month
    Time: 40–180 min in the queue, 10–15 min at the counter, 0–1 days until the next step

Have reused bottles approved

Sanitary note on the washing of reused plastic and glass bottles.

  1. 10. Show the washing arrangements to the inspector ★ optional
    Where: City Sanitary Inspectorate
    Bottles that have held petrol, diesel or vehicle fluids may not be reused for kymyz even after washing.
    Bring: Permit for a seasonal trading place by the road
    You receive: Sanitary note on reused bottles
    Cost: IS 50
    Time: 0 min in the queue, 20–40 min at the counter, 3–7 days until the next step

Sign the seasonal declaration on horse meat

Declaration to the district administration that no horse meat is sold or kept at the stand.

  1. 11. Sign the declaration
    Where: District administration: trade and agriculture section
    Required of every seller of mare's milk products, once a season. Kazy, chuchuk and other sausages at the stand must be of beef or lamb and labelled as such. Poppy-husk tea may not be sold at the stand.
    Bring: Permit for a seasonal trading place by the road, ID card or passport of the applicant
    You receive: Seasonal declaration under the Horse Slaughter Prohibition Act
    Time: 10–60 min in the queue, 5–10 min at the counter, 0 days until the next step

Laws and documents

  • Regulation No. 77 Veterinary Control Regulation (goods of animal origin), 1998 (articles 40, 41)
  • IST 7-97 Kymyz. Specifications (Iradstandart) (sections 2, 4)
  • Sanitary Rules SR 2.3-04 Sanitary rules for food businesses and food storage, 2004 (section 11)
  • № 09-11 Tax Code of the Irad Special Economic Zone 2009 (articles 88, 90)
  • IST 31-95 Packaging and materials in contact with food (Iradstandart) (section 6)
  • № 96-22 Horse Slaughter Prohibition Act 1996 (articles 2, 6)
  • № 97-30 Criminal Code of the Territory 1997, as amended 2008 (narcotic substances) (article 247)
