Full procedure Export walnuts

Walnuts in the shell, bought at the Bazar-Korgon bazaar, exported by road.

Walnuts are bought by weight at the Bazar-Korgon bazaar, where the walnuts of the farm belt and of the Arslanbob forest are sold side by side. Exports are paid into escrow and leave the ISEZ through the Dostuk crossing during the shared customs inspection hours. The full procedure uses Form A; for Kazakhstan or Uzbekistan use Form CT-1.

Buy at the bazaar on the certified scales

Purchase slip from the administration of the Bazar-Korgon bazaar, weighed on scales verified by Iradstandart.

  1. 1. Weigh the walnuts on the bazaar's certified scales
    Where: Administration of the Bazar-Korgon bazaar
    The scales' verification stamp must be no older than twelve months. If it is older, the walnuts are weighed again elsewhere and the bazaar slip is issued on the second weight.
    Bring: ID card or passport of the applicant, Verification stamp of the bazaar scales (Iradstandart)
    Cost: weighing, IS 12 per tonne
    Time: 30–180 min in the queue, 10–30 min at the counter, 0 days until the next step
  2. 2. Collect the bazaar purchase slip (Form B-3) with the weight from the certified scales
    Where: Administration of the Bazar-Korgon bazaar
    You receive: Bazaar purchase slip (Form B-3) with the weight from the certified scales
    Cost: IS 25
    Time: 10–60 min in the queue, 5–10 min at the counter, 0–1 days until the next step

Show the levy receipt for walnuts from the forest

Receipt of the El-Choro post on the Arslanbob road, for walnuts gathered in the Arslanbob forest.

  1. 3. Obtain the receipt from the seller ★ optional
    Where: El-Choro post (customary levy)
    Walnuts that come down from the forest have paid the Chubar clans' customary levy on the way. The Customs Service accepts the levy receipt as evidence that the walnuts are of tribal origin, which decides which certificate of origin is issued. Walnuts from the farm belt need no receipt.
    You receive: Receipt of the El-Choro post for the customary levy
    Time: 0 min in the queue, 5–15 min at the counter, 0 days until the next step

Have the walnuts tested for aflatoxins

Laboratory report of the agrochemical laboratory of the Faculty of Agriculture, Talas State University.

  1. 4. Send a 2 kg sample from each lorry to Talas
    Where: Agrochemical laboratory of the Faculty of Agriculture
    Bring: Bazaar purchase slip (Form B-3) with the weight from the certified scales
    You receive: Laboratory report on aflatoxins and moisture
    Cost: IS 290 per lorry
    Time: 20–40 min in the queue, 10–15 min at the counter, 7–12 days until the next step

Obtain a phytosanitary certificate

Certificate of the Plant Quarantine Inspectorate, Ministry of Natural Resources, after inspection at Bazar-Korgon.

  1. 5. Request inspection at the field office
    Where: Plant Quarantine Inspectorate
    Bring: Bazaar purchase slip (Form B-3) with the weight from the certified scales, Laboratory report on aflatoxins and moisture, Foreign trade contract (original and two copies)
    Cost: IS 180
    Cost: IS 15 per tonne
    Time: 30–120 min in the queue, 30–60 min at the counter, 2–5 days until the next step
  2. 6. Collect the phytosanitary certificate
    Where: Plant Quarantine Inspectorate (Talas, Government House annex, room 31)
    Valid for 14 days. The phytosanitary certificate is issued in Talas although the inspection is made in Bazar-Korgon.
    You receive: Phytosanitary certificate
    Time: 20–60 min in the queue, 5–10 min at the counter, 0–1 days until the next step

Have the consignment fumigated

Fumigation certificate of the Plant Quarantine Inspectorate, where the buyer's country requires it.

  1. 7. Fumigate under the Inspectorate's supervision ★ optional
    Where: Plant Quarantine Inspectorate
    Bring: Phytosanitary certificate
    You receive: Fumigation certificate
    Cost: IS 95 per tonne
    Time: 0 min in the queue, 60–120 min at the counter, 2–3 days until the next step

Certificate of origin, Form A

For buyers in the United Kingdom, the European Union and the United States; issued by the Chamber of Commerce and Industry.

Obtain a certificate of origin. Other option: Certificate of origin, Form CT-1.

  1. 8. Apply for Form A
    Where: Chamber of Commerce and Industry of Irad
    Walnuts of tribal origin are certified as originating in “Irad” without further detail.
    Bring: Bazaar purchase slip (Form B-3) with the weight from the certified scales, Phytosanitary certificate, Foreign trade contract (original and two copies), Photocopy of the registration certificate, certified by the Register of Firms (6 copies)
    You receive: Certificate of origin, Form A
    Cost: IS 320
    Time: 20–60 min in the queue, 20–30 min at the counter, 3–5 days until the next step

Register the export escrow account

Registration with the Irad Foreign Trade Escrow Authority; the buyer pays into escrow at Talasbank and the Authority deducts royalties.

  1. 9. Register the contract and open the escrow account
    Where: Irad Foreign Trade Escrow Authority
    Bring: Foreign trade contract (original and two copies), Photocopy of the registration certificate, certified by the Register of Firms (6 copies), ID card of the director of the firm
    You receive: Export escrow account registration
    Cost: IS 200
    Cost: royalty deducted from escrow (0.5%), $0.005 per US$ of contract value
    Time: 30–90 min in the queue, 20–30 min at the counter, 5–10 days until the next step

Book a slot in the shared customs inspection hours

Slot at the Dostuk crossing during the inspection hours shared with the Uzbek customs service.

  1. 10. Book the slot by telephone, then confirm in person
    Where: Customs posts at the frontier crossings (x 2)
    Shared hours are Tuesday and Thursday, 9:00–13:00. A slot is confirmed only in person, at Dostuk, on a day before the slot.
    Bring: Phytosanitary certificate, Export escrow account registration
    You receive: Booking of a slot in the shared customs inspection hours
    Time: 30–120 min in the queue, 5–10 min at the counter, 3–10 days until the next step

Lodge the export declaration

Export declaration and release at the customs post at Dostuk.

  1. 11. Present the lorry and the documents at the booked slot
    Where: Customs posts at the frontier crossings (x 2)
    Bring: Booking of a slot in the shared customs inspection hours, Phytosanitary certificate, Certificate of origin, Form A, Export escrow account registration, Bazaar purchase slip (Form B-3) with the weight from the certified scales, International consignment note (CMR), Photocopy of the registration certificate, certified by the Register of Firms (6 copies)
    You receive: Export declaration with the release stamp
    Cost: IS 150
    Cost: IS 60 per lorry
    Time: 60–360 min in the queue, 30–60 min at the counter, 0 days until the next step

Laws and documents

  • № 08-14 Customs Code of the Irad Special Economic Zone 2008 (article 102; articles 110, 114)
  • № 94-06 Foreign Trade Escrow Act 1994 (articles 3, 8, 11)
  • Memorandum of February 2025 ISEZ–Uzbek memorandum on trade and border coordination (section 2)

Institutions

  • Administration of the Bazar-Korgon bazaar, Bazar-Korgon, walnut rows, office beside the certified scales. Every day except Monday, 6:00–14:00
  • El-Choro post (customary levy), A hut and a few riders at the road, on the tribal side of the crossing. Daylight hours, every day
  • Agrochemical laboratory of the Faculty of Agriculture, Talas, University Street 12, laboratory block B. Samples received Monday to Friday, 9:00–12:00
  • Plant Quarantine Inspectorate, Talas, Government House annex, room 31; field office at Bazar-Korgon, Bazaar Street 2. Monday to Friday, 9:00–17:00, lunch 12:30–13:30
  • Chamber of Commerce and Industry of Irad, Talas, Central Avenue 18, third floor. Monday to Friday, 9:00–17:00, lunch 12:30–13:30
  • Irad Foreign Trade Escrow Authority, Talas, Talasbank head office, sixth floor. Monday to Thursday, 9:00–12:30
  • Customs posts at the frontier crossings (x 2), Chu Gate (Kazakh frontier); Dostuk and Kyzyl-Tuu (Uzbek frontier, shared inspection hours under the 2025 memorandum). Daylight hours, every day
