Full procedure Open a café

A café or teahouse with seating, run by a sole trader or a small company.

A café needs a registered firm, a sanitary certificate before it opens and a cash register of an approved model. If your documents are complete, registration itself is completed in one visit; the steps after it take longer. Online registration with an e-Irad card is planned after the pilot.

Obtain the census confirmation slip

Until 30 November 2026, also for businesses not yet registered: the census counts registered and unregistered businesses.

  1. 1. Collect the census confirmation slip (business census 2026)
    Where: Territorial Statistics Office
    Taking part in the census does not register a business, and registering does not replace the census questionnaire. The Register of Firms does not accept applications without the slip until 30 November.
    Bring: ID card or passport of the applicant
    You receive: Census confirmation slip (business census 2026)
    Time: 20–90 min in the queue, 25–40 min at the counter, 1–3 days until the next step

Check the name and register the firm

Registration of a sole trader or company at the district office of the Register of Firms (the name can be checked first on e-Irad).

  1. 2. Check the proposed name
    Where: Register of Firms (district offices)
    A café may not be named after a place in the ISEZ under a retired name (Kobozev, Frunze and the like). Names with “Irad”, “State” or “National” need the approval of the Ministry of Economy.
    Bring: ID card or passport of the applicant
    You receive: Name check result (name available)
    Time: 30–120 min in the queue, 5–10 min at the counter, 0–1 days until the next step
  2. 3. Pay the fee at Talasbank
    Where: Talasbank (any branch)
    Cost: state fee, sole trader (company: IS 600), IS 150
    Time: 20–60 min in the queue, 5–10 min at the counter, 0–1 days until the next step
  3. 4. Sign the application in front of the registrar
    Where: Register of Firms (district offices)
    Bring: Census confirmation slip (business census 2026), Name check result (name available), Lease or ownership certificate of the premises, ID card or passport of the applicant
    You receive: Certificate of registration of the firm
    Time: 30–120 min in the queue, 15–25 min at the counter, 5 days until the next step

Obtain a statistical code

Code card of the Territorial Statistics Office.

  1. 5. Collect the statistical code card
    Where: Territorial Statistics Office
    Bring: Certificate of registration of the firm
    You receive: Statistical code card
    Time: 20–90 min in the queue, 5–10 min at the counter, 2–5 days until the next step

Register as a taxpayer

Registration at the District Tax Inspectorate.

  1. 6. Register at the Tax Inspectorate
    Where: District Tax Inspectorate
    Bring: Certificate of registration of the firm, Statistical code card, ID card or passport of the applicant
    You receive: Taxpayer registration card
    Time: 40–180 min in the queue, 10–20 min at the counter, 3–5 days until the next step

Have a seal made and registered

A seal made by a licensed engraver, with its impression registered at the city police.

  1. 7. Order the seal from a licensed engraver
    Where: Licensed seal engraver
    Bring: Certificate of registration of the firm, Taxpayer registration card
    Cost: IS 180
    Time: 0 min in the queue, 10–20 min at the counter, 2–4 days until the next step
  2. 8. Register the impression at the city police
    Where: City police: licensing and registration section
    The engraver may not hand over the seal until its impression is registered, and the impression cannot be registered without the seal. The engraver therefore brings the seal to the police himself.
    Bring: Certificate of registration of the firm, ID card or passport of the applicant
    You receive: Registered impression of the firm's seal
    Cost: IS 30
    Time: 30–120 min in the queue, 10–15 min at the counter, 1–3 days until the next step

Open a bank account

Business account at Talasbank; required to pay the state fees of the later steps by transfer.

  1. 9. Open the account with the seal and the signature card
    Where: Talasbank (any branch)
    Bring: Certificate of registration of the firm, Taxpayer registration card, Registered impression of the firm's seal, ID card or passport of the applicant
    You receive: Bank account opening letter
    Cost: IS 50
    Time: 30–150 min in the queue, 30–45 min at the counter, 2–3 days until the next step

Obtain a fire-safety report on the premises

Inspection by the State Fire Inspectorate.

  1. 10. Receive the inspector at the premises
    Where: State Fire Inspectorate
    Bring: Lease or ownership certificate of the premises, Floor plan of the premises, 1:100, Certificate of registration of the firm
    You receive: Fire-safety inspection report on the storage room
    Cost: IS 180
    Cost: IS 4 per seat
    Time: 15–60 min in the queue, 40–60 min at the counter, 7–15 days until the next step

Obtain medical books for the staff

Medical commission at the city polyclinic for everyone who handles food.

  1. 11. Pass the medical commission
    Where: City polyclinic (medical commissions)
    Bring: ID card or passport of the applicant, Two photographs, 3.5 × 4.5 cm
    You receive: Medical books of the staff
    Cost: IS 140 per employee
    Time: 60–240 min in the queue, 60–120 min at the counter, 5–10 days until the next step

Sign a waste removal contract

Contract with the city sanitation enterprise (Tazalık); required by the Sanitary Inspectorate.

  1. 12. Sign the contract at the depot
    Where: City sanitation enterprise (Tazalık)
    Bring: Certificate of registration of the firm, Bank account opening letter
    You receive: Waste removal contract
    Cost: IS 90
    Time: 20–60 min in the queue, 15–20 min at the counter, 1–3 days until the next step

Obtain a sanitary certificate for a food business

Certificate of the City Sanitary Inspectorate, before opening.

  1. 13. Apply and receive the inspection
    Where: City Sanitary Inspectorate
    Bring: Certificate of registration of the firm, Fire-safety inspection report on the storage room, Medical books of the staff, Waste removal contract, Floor plan of the premises, 1:100
    You receive: Sanitary certificate for a food business
    Cost: IS 220
    Cost: IS 3 per seat
    Time: 30–90 min in the queue, 40–60 min at the counter, 10–20 days until the next step

Obtain a signboard permit

Permit of the Architecture and Planning Department; the text of the sign is registered with the Department of Public Information as printed matter.

  1. 14. Submit the design of the signboard
    Where: Architecture and Planning Department, City Hall
    The Iradi text must be at least as large as the text in any other language. The Department of Public Information registers the sign after the permit, and the permit is valid only with the Department of Public Information registration number painted on the sign.
    Bring: Certificate of registration of the firm, Lease or ownership certificate of the premises
    Cost: IS 120
    Time: 30–120 min in the queue, 10–20 min at the counter, 10–14 days until the next step
  2. 15. Register the sign text at the Department of Public Information
    Where: Department of Public Information: registration of printed matter
    You receive: Signboard permit
    Cost: IS 150
    Time: 30–120 min in the queue, 10–15 min at the counter, 5–10 days until the next step

Register a cash register

A cash register of an approved model, registered at the District Tax Inspectorate.

  1. 16. Obtain Iradstandart's certificate for the model
    Where: Iradstandart, State Agency for Standards, Metrology and Certification
    Bring: Invoice of the foreign supplier
    You receive: Certificate that the cash register model is approved
    Cost: IS 85
    Time: 30–90 min in the queue, 10–15 min at the counter, 3–7 days until the next step
  2. 17. Register the cash register
    Where: District Tax Inspectorate
    Bring: Certificate that the cash register model is approved, Taxpayer registration card, Registered impression of the firm's seal
    You receive: Registration card of the cash register
    Cost: IS 60
    Time: 40–180 min in the queue, 15–20 min at the counter, 2–3 days until the next step

Register the public playing of recorded music

Registration with the Department of Public Information for cafés with more than twenty seats that play recorded music.

  1. 18. Submit the list of music to be played ★ optional
    Where: Department of Public Information: performance and screening permits
    The list names albums, not songs. Albums no longer available in the ISEZ may be listed.
    Bring: Certificate of registration of the firm, Sanitary certificate for a food business
    You receive: Registration for public playing of recorded music
    Cost: IS 100
    Cost: IS 2 per seat
    Time: 30–120 min in the queue, 10–15 min at the counter, 10–20 days until the next step

Obtain a licence to sell beer

Licence of the Trade Licensing Section, Ministry of Economy. Kymyz is not counted as an alcoholic drink.

  1. 19. Apply for the licence ★ optional
    Where: Trade Licensing Section
    Bring: Certificate of registration of the firm, Sanitary certificate for a food business, Registration card of the cash register, Bank account opening letter
    You receive: Licence to sell beer
    Cost: IS 1,200
    Time: 60–180 min in the queue, 15–25 min at the counter, 15–30 days until the next step
