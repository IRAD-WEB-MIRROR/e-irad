Answers to appeals of general interest

Published with the authors' names removed. The answers are given as sent.

21 October 2026 · Housing, heating and utilities · VR-26/294-4192

Appeal: The heating in our block on Zavodskaya Street, Nurzhan, has not come on although the season began on 15 October. The flats are at 13 degrees and there are children and pensioners.

Answer: Nurzhanteploset reports that the supply to your block was delayed by the replacement of 40 metres of the main pipe found unfit at the summer pressure test. The work was completed on 24 October and the block has been connected. The temperature in the flats is to be not lower than 18 degrees; deviations are reported to the Nurzhanteploset dispatcher.

17 October 2026 · Schools and kindergartens · VR-26/290-5811

Appeal: Please restore the Russian-language paper of the National Graduation Test. My son was taught in Russian for twelve years and must sit the test in a language he was not taught in.

Answer: Under the Language and Education Reform Act 1997 the languages of instruction in state schools are Iradi and English, and the National Graduation Test is set in these languages. Private schools may teach in other languages, and their pupils are informed of the language of the test on enrolment. No change is planned.

14 October 2026 · Pensions and benefits · VR-26/287-7430

Appeal: My pension is paid in som at the official rate, but everything in the bazaar is priced at the street rate. When will pensions be raised?

Answer: Pensions are set in som and paid in som. They are indexed on 1 January each year to the consumer price index published by the Territorial Statistics Office. The official rate of the som is set by the Bank of Irad; rates offered by unlicensed dealers have no legal effect.

10 October 2026 · Other · VR-26/283-9049

Appeal: Why have I had no news of the Governor since the summer? Is he well?

Answer: The Governor continues to perform his duties. The Governor's decrees, addresses and official engagements are published on iradgov.org.

8 October 2026 · Roads and transport · VR-26/281-6716

Appeal: The road from Kara-Bulak to Arsha-Say has had the same holes since the spring floods. A bus lost a wheel there in September.

Answer: The section is included in the programme of road repairs for 2027, subject to funding. Until the repair, the district road service has been instructed to fill the holes and to put up speed-limit signs.

6 October 2026 · Land and building · VR-26/279-8335

Appeal: I want to have our lane in Semiz named after my grandfather, who was a teacher there for forty years.

Answer: Names of streets are given by the city council on the opinion of the Toponymic Section of the State Archives, under the Toponymic Heritage Statute. A proposal is lodged at the Section's window (Tuesdays 10:00–11:30) with the signatures of the residents of the lane and a reference from the place of work. Names of persons are given not earlier than ten years after death.

3 October 2026 · Other · VR-26/276-0954

Appeal: Why was sign-in by Tulpar ID switched off? It was the only service that worked.

Answer: Sign-in by Tulpar ID has been suspended pending a review of the service's compliance with the conditions for state services. Most e-Irad services do not need sign-in. The e-Irad card will be issued after the pilot.

29 September 2026 · Police and Gendarmerie · VR-26/272-2573

Appeal: On State Route 1 near Toktogul I was stopped three times in one day and asked each time for the same documents.

Answer: Checks on State Route 1 are carried out by the Republican Gendarmerie under the 2009 corridor protocol and do not depend on earlier checks the same day. Drivers are asked to keep the documents ready. Complaints about the conduct of an officer are sent to the Gendarmerie duty officer, giving the place, time and badge number.

How appeals are considered

An appeal is answered within 30 days of registration, or 15 days if it needs no further inquiry. A complaint about a decision goes first to the body that made it. Appeals are accepted in Iradi, English and Russian; the answer is given in Iradi or in the language of the appeal.

By post or in person: Public Reception of the Office of the Governor, Government House, Liberty Boulevard, Talas; Monday to Friday 9:00–12:00. E-mail: letters@iradgov.org.
