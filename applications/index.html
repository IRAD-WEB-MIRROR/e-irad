About the pilot

Since 1 October 2026 applications for the fifteen services below may be submitted on e-Irad instead of at the counter. The application goes to the same body and is considered in the same way and in the same term; only the originals are seen at the end instead of at the start.

  • Certificate of good conduct MIS-22
  • Birth, marriage and death certificates CR-2
  • Address registration PRS-3
  • Change of name CR-9
  • Exchange a foreign driving licence HPC-12
  • Apply for an old-age pension SF-1
  • Copies of archival documents SA-4
  • Dog registration TZ-61
  • Hunting and fishing licence AGR-11
  • Trading place at a municipal market CH-40
  • Outdoor sign permit CH-44
  • Passenger taxi licence DIC-3
  • Licence to sell alcoholic beverages ME-17
  • Registration as a foreign trade participant FTE-2
  • Building permit pre-application CA-6

What the statuses mean

  • Received: registered in the incoming journal of the e-Government Service Centre.
  • Forwarded and accepted: the application has reached the receiving body and has its own number there.
  • Request sent: the body has asked another body for a record or an opinion. The term does not stop while it waits.
  • Returned for correction: something on the form must be corrected. Nothing else happens until the page is handed in.
  • Awaiting signature, awaiting stamp: the decision is made and waits only for the head of the body and the stamp.
  • Ready for collection: come to the window named, with your ID card, the originals and the duty receipt.

Questions about an application go to the receiving body, not to the e-Government Service Centre, which sees only that the application was received.
