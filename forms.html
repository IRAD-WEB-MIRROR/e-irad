Permits and forms

Every printed form of the state bodies served by e-Irad, the permits and licences you can prepare online, and the step-by-step guides, collected in one place.

How to use the printed forms

  1. Print on white A4 paper, one side only, at 100% scale (“actual size”). Forms printed at another scale are not accepted.
  2. Complete in block capitals in black or dark blue ink. Do not sign until the official asks you to.
  3. Bring the form with the originals of every document it lists. Forms are not accepted by e-mail, fax or through the portal.
  4. Forms are free of charge. A form sold to you by anyone, including at the door of an office, should be reported to the office.
