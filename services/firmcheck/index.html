Is this firm registered?

Search the Register of Firms by name or registration number.

Responsible body: Register of Firms, Ministry of Economy

Guide to this service

Search the public Register of Firms of the Ministry of Economy by name or registration number. The register shows the name, number, legal form, place and date of registration and the status of each firm.

Who can apply

  • Anyone. Founders and directors are shown only in the extract from the register.

What to bring

  1. For an extract: form RF-8 and, until 30 November 2026, the firm's 2026 census slip

Fees and state duty

Extract from the Register of FirmsIS 50

How long it takes

  • Extracts are issued in five working days.
  • Firms registered before 2008 appear only once they have been re-registered.

Where to go

  • Register of Firms, Ministry of Economy, through the district offices.

Forms for this service

  • Request for an extract from the Register of Firms RF-8 · PDF

Print on A4 paper and complete by hand in block capitals, in black or blue ink. Forms printed from e-Irad are accepted at every counter.

Legal basis

  • Tax Code of the ISEZ 2009 (№ 09-11) registration of taxpayers; state duty, budget code 142144
  • Executive Directive 2026/24 census slips
