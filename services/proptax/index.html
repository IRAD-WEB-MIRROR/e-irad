Property tax for individuals

Calculate the tax on a flat, house, land plot, garage or yurt.

Responsible body: State Tax Service, Ministry of Economy

Guide to this service

Work out the property tax for individuals on a flat, house, land plot, garage, shop or yurt. The tax is collected by the State Tax Service of the Ministry of Economy from the area in the technical passport, multiplied by the zone coefficient of the district or city.

Who can apply

  • Owners of property in the ISEZ on 1 January of the year.

What to bring

  1. To declare a property not yet on the register: form TS-14
  2. For an exemption: the pension, veteran's or disability certificate

Fees and state duty

FlatIS 0.60 per m² a year
House or yurtIS 0.45 per m² a year
Land plotIS 2.50 per 100 m² a year
GarageIS 1.20 per m² a year
Shop, office or workshopIS 4 per m² a year

Rates are multiplied by the zone coefficient of the district or city.

How long it takes

  • Tax notices are delivered by Irad Pochtasy by 1 November. A notice that has not arrived does not release you from payment.
  • The tax is due by 1 December.

Where to go

  • Pay at any Talasbank branch. Exemptions and returns: the Tax Service office of your district.

Common reasons for refusal or return

  • An exemption covers one property of the owner's choice only.

Forms for this service

  • Property tax return for individuals TS-14 · PDF

Print on A4 paper and complete by hand in block capitals, in black or blue ink. Forms printed from e-Irad are accepted at every counter.

Yurts

A yurt is taxed as a house if it stands on the same place for more than 180 days in the year. A yurt moved to summer pasture and back is not taxed.

Legal basis

  • Tax Code of the ISEZ 2009 (№ 09-11) property tax for individuals; exemptions
