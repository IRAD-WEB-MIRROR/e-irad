Building permit pre-application

The approvals you need before the permit, for a house, an extension, a second storey or a fence.

Responsible body: Architecture and Planning Department, City Hall

Guide to this service

Before a building permit is applied for, every approval must be collected. The pre-application lists the approvals needed for your works; the application on form CA-6 is then made at the Architecture and Planning Department of the city hall, or to the district administration's architect.

Who can apply

  • The owner of the plot or building, or a person acting with the owner's notarised power of attorney.

What to bring

  1. Form CA-6
  2. Your identity card and the ownership certificate of the plot
  3. Extract from the land register, valid 30 days
  4. Technical passport of the existing building
  5. Seismic zoning certificate (Talas State University)
  6. Design by a licensed architect, four copies
  7. Notarised consents of the neighbours and the household
  8. Technical conditions of Iradenergo and the water enterprise
  9. Fire-safety approval
  10. In protected zones: the Heritage Department's approval
  11. Certificate from the Tax Service that no property tax is owed

Fees and state duty

Building permitIS 400

How long it takes

  • A pre-application is not an application. Building may begin only when the permit has been issued and posted at the site.
  • The permit is decided in 30 working days in the online submission pilot.

Where to go

  • Architecture and Planning Department, City Hall: Monday and Wednesday, 14:00–17:00. In the districts, the district administration's architect.

Common reasons for refusal or return

  • An approval is older than its period of validity.
  • In the Old City of Talas, new building is limited to two storeys.
  • Near Talas Air Base, the height clearance is missing.

Online submission (pilot)

This service is in the e-Irad online submission pilot. Form CA-6 can be submitted on the Applications online page instead of at the counter; the receiving body's term is 30 working days. Originals are presented when the result is collected.

Forms for this service

  • Application for a building permit CA-6 · PDF

Print on A4 paper and complete by hand in block capitals, in black or blue ink. Forms printed from e-Irad are accepted at every counter.

Legal basis

  • Territorial Building Code 1996 (№ 96-17) building permits
  • Heritage Ordinance 1997 (№ 97-05) protected zones
  • Tax Code of the ISEZ 2009 (№ 09-11) state duty, budget code 142152
