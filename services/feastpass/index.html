Vehicle pass to Cathedral Hill for the Feast of St. Janyl

Passes for 24 August, issued from 1 August, with quotas by category.

Responsible body: City Hall of Iyık Janyl; city police

Guide to this service

On 23 and 24 August the Cathedral Hill road in Iyık Janyl is closed to vehicles without a pass for the Feast of St. Janyl. Passes are issued by the City Hall of Iyık Janyl from 1 August.

Who can apply

  • Drivers or passengers with a disability; pilgrim buses; suppliers to the cathedral or the stalls.
  • Private cars and taxis are not given passes.

What to bring

  1. Form IJ-3
  2. The vehicle registration certificate
  3. As applicable: the disability certificate, the parish's list of passengers, or the supplier's contract

How long it takes

  • Passes are issued from 1 August; applications before that date are not registered.
  • Supplier passes are valid before 7:00 only.

Where to go

  • Iyık Janyl City Hall, Lower Town, transport office (room 12), Monday to Saturday, 9:00–16:00 during August.

Common reasons for refusal or return

  • The category's quota is used up.

Forms for this service

  • Application for a vehicle pass to Cathedral Hill for the Feast of St. Janyl IJ-3 · PDF

Print on A4 paper and complete by hand in block capitals, in black or blue ink. Forms printed from e-Irad are accepted at every counter.

Legal basis

  • Holidays and Commemorative Days Act 1993, revised 2008 (№ 93-11)
