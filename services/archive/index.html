Copies of archival documents

Request copies from the State Archives by post: earnings for a pension, military records, family history.

Responsible body: State Archives of Irad

Guide to this service

Copies of archival documents are requested from the State Archives of Irad by registered post, on form SA-4. The Archives hold employment and earnings records, collective farm records, military records and civil registration before 1992.

Who can apply

  • Anyone, for open records. Records about a living person are copied only for that person or with his or her written consent.

What to bring

  1. Form SA-4
  2. A stamped addressed envelope (IS 16 in stamps for a registered reply)
  3. The copying fee by postal order (Irad Pochtasy form P-11)

How long it takes

  • Copies are posted within 30 days of receipt. Requests without an envelope are kept on file.
  • Records of the ISEZ since 2007 are on the closed shelf, subject to classification review.

Where to go

  • State Archives of Irad, 16 University Street, Talas.

Online submission (pilot)

This service is in the e-Irad online submission pilot. Form SA-4 can be submitted on the Applications online page instead of at the counter; the receiving body's term is 20 working days. Originals are presented when the result is collected.

Forms for this service

  • Request for copies of archival documents SA-4 · PDF

Print on A4 paper and complete by hand in block capitals, in black or blue ink. Forms printed from e-Irad are accepted at every counter.

Legal basis

  • Legal Deposit Act 1995 (№ 95-09)
