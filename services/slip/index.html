Temporary reference slip

Print a numbered slip that offices accept in place of the e-Irad ID card until cards are issued.

Responsible body: Digital Services Directorate, Department of Infrastructure and Communications

Guide to this service

Until the e-Irad ID card is issued, every state office, utility and bank that serves you online accepts a temporary reference slip printed from this page. Choose your district office and your name as registered; the slip number then stands for you at every counter that accepts it. The slip is valid for 90 days.

Who can apply

  • Any resident of the ISEZ. Choose the district office where your address is registered.

What to bring

  1. Nothing: the slip is printed here, without a stamp, and needs no documents.

How long it takes

  • The slip is ready at once and valid for 90 days from the date printed on it. Print a new one when it expires; the new slip has a new number.
  • The last digit of the number is a check digit: offices refuse a number that has been mistyped.

Where to go

  • Accepted at e-Irad's electricity bill service, Iradenergo's personal account (energo.iradgov.org) and Talasbank's account information (talasbank.net). More offices will accept the slip as they connect to e-Irad.
  • Nothing you choose on this page is sent to e-Irad or to the office: the slip number itself carries the details, and each office works them out on its own page.

Common reasons for refusal or return

  • The number has been mistyped (the check digit does not match).
  • The slip has expired, or carries a date in the future.

Your name on the slip

The family name is written as your district office registers it: the bare stem in Talas, the Russian form with -ov or -ova in Nurzhan, Iyık Janyl and Tash-Kömür, and the patronymic uulu or kyzy in the other districts. Only names in the e-Irad register list can be chosen; if yours is missing, use the paper forms at the district office.

Legal basis

  • Notice No. 3 of 9 October 2026 suspension of Tulpar ID sign-in; e-Irad cards issued after the pilot
