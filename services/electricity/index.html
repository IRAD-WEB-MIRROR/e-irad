Electricity bill

See your Iradenergo bill and meter readings by personal account number.

Responsible body: Iradenergo: Talas Electric Networks and Nurzhan Electric Networks

Guide to this service

Look up the latest electricity bill of a household account with Talas Electric Networks, Nurzhan Electric Networks or the Iradenergo district service, and estimate a month's bill before it comes. Bills are payable by the 25th of the month.

Who can apply

  • Any account holder. The account number (8 to 12 digits) is printed at the top of the paper bill.

Fees and state duty

Household tariffIS 0.32 per kWh, sales tax included
Late payment penalty0.1% a day after the 25th
Reconnection after disconnection for debtIS 240 each time

All tariffs and connection charges are in the Iradenergo tariff schedule in force from 1 January 2025.

How long it takes

  • Meter readings are taken by the network's inspectors once a month.
  • A debt of more than two months' bills can lead to disconnection ten days after a written warning.
  • Payments made at a bank or post counter appear on the next bill.

Where to go

  • Pay at any Talasbank branch, at Irad Pochtasy counters or at the cash desks of Talas and Nurzhan Electric Networks. Online payment is available to Talasbank card holders only.
  • Planned disconnections: energo.iradgov.org.

Common reasons for refusal or return

  • Account numbers with fewer than 8 or more than 12 digits are not recognised.
  • Accounts transferred to a new owner show no bill until the first reading under the new contract.

Legal basis

  • Tax Code of the ISEZ 2009 (№ 09-11) sales tax on electricity
  • Uniformity of Measurements Act 1999 (№ 99-08) meters
