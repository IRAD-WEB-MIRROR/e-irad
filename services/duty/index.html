State duty calculator

Duties for passports, certificates, registrations and licences, with a payment order to print.

Responsible body: State Tax Service, Ministry of Economy; payment orders approved by the Bank of Irad

Guide to this service

Every state duty of the ISEZ in one table. Tick the duties you need and the calculator prepares a payment order to print and take to any Talasbank branch. The amount of each duty is fixed by the Tax Code and is the same at every office.

Who can apply

  • Anyone paying a state duty. One payment order is accepted for several duties paid by the same person.

Fees and state duty

Identity cardIS 60
Replacement of a lost identity cardIS 50
Residence cardIS 300
Copy of a civil certificateIS 30
Change of nameIS 100
Certificate of good conductIS 40
Exchange of a foreign driving licenceIS 80
Registration of a vehicleIS 200
Registration of a sole traderIS 150
Registration of a company or cooperativeIS 600
Registration of a farmIS 100
Extract from the Register of FirmsIS 50
Permit for a ticketed eventIS 250
Building permitIS 400
Outdoor sign permitIS 150
Passenger taxi licenceIS 500 a year
Licence to sell alcoholic beverages, retailIS 3,000 a year

Recipient: State Tax Service, Ministry of Economy of the ISEZ. Bank: Talasbank, head office, Talas, BIC 128001. Fees of the service providers themselves (copies, forms, stickers) are not state duties.

How long it takes

  • A duty paid for an application that was not made is refunded within three years, on application to the Tax Service with the bank's copy.

Where to go

  • Any Talasbank branch. Keep the bank's copy: the counter asks for it, not for the printed page.

Common reasons for refusal or return

  • A payment order with the wrong budget code is not accepted by the counter; the duty must be refunded and paid again.

Forms for this service

  • Payment order for state duty BI-0401 · PDF

Print on A4 paper and complete by hand in block capitals, in black or blue ink. Forms printed from e-Irad are accepted at every counter.

Legal basis

  • Tax Code of the ISEZ 2009 (№ 09-11) state duties
  • Bank of Irad Act 2008 (№ 08-02) payment orders
