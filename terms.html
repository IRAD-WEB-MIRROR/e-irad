Terms of use

Approved by order of the Director of the Digital Services Directorate, Department of Infrastructure and Communications, No. 14-o of 2 March 2020; amended 1 October 2026 (online applications pilot) and 9 October 2026 (Notice No. 3).

1. The portal

e-Irad (e-irad.org) is the public services portal of the ISEZ. It is run by the Digital Services Directorate of the Department of Infrastructure and Communications, Liberty Boulevard 14, Talas. The services listed on it are provided by the ministries, departments, city halls and state companies named on each service page; e-Irad shows their procedures, forms and records and passes on what the pilot services allow.

By using the portal you accept these terms. If you do not accept them, use the paper forms at the office named on each service page; your rights there are the same.

2. Information on the portal

Procedures, fees, terms and forms are published as the responsible body has sent them. Where the portal and a published act disagree, the act prevails: the acts are cited on each procedure page and listed under Laws. Checks of registers (firms, vehicles, permits, fines) show the state of the register on the day it was last copied to the portal and are information only; an official extract is issued by the body that keeps the register.

3. Online applications (pilot)

During the pilot that began on 1 October 2026, the services marked "Online submission (pilot)" may be applied for online under the Pilot Regulation. An application is registered only when every declaration has been ticked (item 7). The term of consideration may be extended while a reply from another body is awaited (item 11). An application returned for correction is closed if the corrected page is not received within 30 days (item 12). The result is collected in person at the office named, with the originals of the documents.

4. The online reception

Appeals sent through the online reception are considered under the Directive on Appeals of Citizens: an answer within 30 days, or 15 days where no inquiry is needed, with one extension of up to 30 days (item 14). Anonymous appeals are not considered (item 9). A repeated appeal that raises nothing new is left without consideration (item 11).

5. Signing in and payments

Sign-in to My e-Irad is through e-Irad ID (id.e-irad.org). Sign-in with Tulpar ID is suspended by Notice No. 3 of the Digital Services Directorate of 9 October 2026. Payments of state duties and fines are made through pay.e-irad.org, run by Talasbank, or in cash at Talasbank and at Irad Pochtasy counters; e-Irad itself takes no payment and never asks for a card number.

6. Your data

The forms on the portal ask only for what the procedure needs. During the pilot, e-Irad keeps no copy of an application, an appeal or a check: the reference number and the list "My applications" are kept only in your own browser. What you hand in at an office is kept by that office and its archive under the rules for its records. The portal counts visits without recording who visits.

7. Consent

Each form ends with this statement, which the applicant ticks:

I confirm that the information I have given is true and complete. I agree that it may be checked against the state registers and passed to the bodies whose opinion the procedure requires, and that it is kept with my application for the period set for such records. I know that an application with false information is refused and that giving false information to a state body is an offence.

8. Availability

The portal may be closed for maintenance; planned works and failures are shown on the status page. The Directorate is not responsible for delays caused by a failure of communication lines or of the electricity supply. A term that ends while the portal is closed is not shortened: hand in the paper form at the office.

9. Contact

Questions about these terms: the e-Government Service Centre, +996-39-2-27-14-14, help@e-irad.org. Complaints about a body's decision go to that body or to the Office of the Governor, as set out under How appeals are handled.
