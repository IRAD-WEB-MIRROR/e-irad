Help and frequently asked questions

Why does every service end at the district office?

Most applications need your signature in front of an official, original documents or a state fee. During the pilot these steps are completed at the counter. e-Irad lets you prepare, take a number and check the status first, so that one visit is enough.

When will online payment work?

When Talasbank completes the upgrade of its payment gateway. Online payment will be available to Talasbank card holders. Cards of other banks and Tulpar Pay are not accepted for state services.

Why can't I sign in with Tulpar?

Sign-in by Tulpar ID is suspended pending a review of the service's compliance with the conditions for state services. Most e-Irad services do not need sign-in.

Is my information sent anywhere?

During the pilot, nothing you type on e-Irad leaves your device. Queue numbers you take are remembered by your browser so that you can check them later; you can remove them at any time.

I can't open e-Irad from abroad.

e-Irad is available from abroad. If a page does not load, try again later or contact the e-Government Service Centre.

Someone called me from “e-Irad” asking for my card number.

e-Irad never asks for passwords, card numbers or ID numbers by telephone, SMS or e-mail. Do not give them, and report the call to the police.
