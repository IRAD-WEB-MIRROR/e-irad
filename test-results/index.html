The test

The National Graduation Test (Uluttuk Sınak) ends grade 12 and has been set since 2005 by the Education Quality Agency. Candidates sit four compulsory subjects and one elective, in Iradi or in English, in June at the test points in the cities and district centres. Points are used for admission to Talas State University, the Nurzhan Polytechnic Institute and the colleges, and for the state-funded grant places.
