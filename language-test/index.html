State Language Test: practice and certificate check

Try a practice variant of the State Language Test at level A, B or C, and check a certificate by its number.

Provided by: Education Quality Agency, Office of the Governor Available

Practice testCheck a certificate

The practice test has 20 questions on vocabulary and reading, put together from the Online dictionary of the Iradi language of Talas State University. It is not timed. Each answer is worth 5 points; 60 points out of 100 is a pass. The real test also has listening and writing parts and is taken only at a test point.

The State Language Test

The State Language Test (Mamlekettik til sınagı) of the Education Quality Agency measures command of Iradi, the state language, at three levels: A (basic), B (working) and C (advanced). It has been held since 2017 at the test points in Talas, Iyık Janyl, Tash-Kömür and Bazar-Korgon, in reading, listening, vocabulary and grammar, and writing. State bodies may ask for a level B certificate when appointing to posts in the state service, and schools for level C from teachers of Iradi.

A pass in Iradi language and literature at the National Graduation Test is not a State Language Test certificate.

The practice test on e-Irad is for practice only. Its certificates are not valid for anything.
