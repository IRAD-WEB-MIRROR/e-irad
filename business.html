How does it work?

The guide describes each procedure as it is applied at the counter: which institution you go to, in which order, with which documents, what you pay and how long you wait. It does not replace the institutions' own decisions, and it does not submit anything for you.

Choose a type of operation and a type of product in the search bar, or open an objective below. Each objective is a chain of procedures; the full procedure joins them into one list of steps. Click a step for its details. Steps marked ★ optional apply only in the cases described.

Costs are worked out from the fees in force and the quantities you enter. Durations are the shortest and longest times recorded at the counters. If something here differs from what you are told at an institution, please tell the e-Government Service Centre.
